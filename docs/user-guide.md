## 1. Creating a Project
Navigate to **New Movie** from the sidebar or click **+ New Movie** in the top navigation:
- Enter your **Movie Title** and **Logline**.
- Choose your **Aspect Ratio**:
  - `16:9`: Cinema & YouTube widescreen
  - `9:16`: TikTok, Reels & Shorts
  - `2.39:1`: Ultra-widescreen anamorphic cinematic
- Select your primary language and regional dialect.

---

## 2. Screenplay Analysis & Dialect Preservation
In **Script Studio**, paste your complete screenplay or story:
- **Chittagong / Chatgaiya Preservation:** CineForge recognizes regional vocabulary (such as `অনরে, হনডে, খাইস্যন, গম আছননি, বেয়াইগ্গুন`) and guarantees that authentic spoken cadences will not be flattened into standard literary Bengali.
- Click **Analyze Script** to generate:
  - 3-Act Story Arc
  - Character Profiles
  - Location Breakdowns
  - Scene divisions with suggested shots and durations
- Click **Commit to Storyboard Engine** to push all scenes into production.

---

## 3. Character Studio & Consistency Locks
To prevent character face drift between cuts:
1. Open **Character Studio** and select a character.
2. Upload a clear **Primary Face Anchor** photo.
3. Click **LOCK CHARACTER**.
4. The character's facial bone structure, skin appearance, hair texture, and signature costume are compiled into descriptive fingerprint anchors for every prompt.

---

## 4. Environment Studio & Spatial Mapping
1. Define architectural materials, lighting moods, and key props.
2. Upload wide establishing angles and interior reference photos.
3. Click **LOCK ENVIRONMENT** to ensure that furniture and background objects do not shift between cuts.

---

## 5. Storyboard Engine & Continuity Checking
In **Storyboard Studio**:
- Reorder shots with **Move Up / Down**.
- Use **Split Shot** to subdivide long takes into dynamic close-ups and reaction shots.
- Review the **Continuity Health Score** (Character Consistency, Costume Preservation, and Narrative Pacing).

---

## 6. Scene Generation & Prompt Formats
In **Scene Generator**, inspect the 5 prompt formats created by the Cinematic Prompt Engine:
1. **Flow Prompt:** Compact and weighted for Google Flow.
2. **Detailed Cinematic Prompt:** Full director's vision with style, lighting, camera kinematics, and negative constraints.
3. **Short Prompt:** Condensed snapshot for fast iterations.
4. **Manual Copy Block:** Clean formatting ready for clipboard.
5. **Official API JSON:** Machine-readable payload for external API connections.

---

## 7. Voice Studio & Foley Audio Studio
- **Voice Studio:** Assign vocal pitches and speeds to each character. Audition sample lines with the browser synthesis preview or record custom actor dialogue via microphone.
- **Audio Studio:** Multi-track mixer with sliders for Dialogue, BGM, Environmental Ambience, and Foley sound effects (synthesized risers, village breezes, and footsteps).

---

## 8. Cinematic Editor & Master MP4 Export
- Import approved storyboard shots into the multi-track timeline.
- Review burned-in subtitle previews with timecode sync.
- Click **Export Master Movie** to render a high-definition MP4 directly onto your system.
